About

Florian Egger

I write science fiction about people caught inside systems that were built without them in mind — ships, armies, orders that keep running long after anyone remembers why.

My first novel, Lifestock, follows two people at opposite ends of a generation ship moving toward the same moment. The next, Ashground, strands a soldier who traded away his empathy on a planet that does not care what he was built for. It arrives in 2027.
